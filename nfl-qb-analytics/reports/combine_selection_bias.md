# Combine selection bias (Phase 5.5)

Generated by `python -m src.modeling.combine_audit`. Population: the Phase-5 verified prospect universe (556 final seasons, 138 drafted). Backtest: test drafts 2014-2025, training on earlier drafts only, every variant scored on the same 384 rows (87 drafted).

## Answer

* **Most of the Combine gain is participation, not measurement.** Adding only the participation flag (B) moves AUC from 0.878 to 0.906, 97% of the full gain to 0.907 (C). Brier changes by -0.0196 (95% class-bootstrap interval -0.0345 to -0.0056).
* Adding forty time, height and weight on top of the flag (B to C) changes Brier by -0.0038 (-0.0091 to +0.0013).
* Measurements **without** the flag (D, non-participants median-imputed) change Brier by +0.0018 against college-only (-0.0027 to +0.0064): **no improvement**. Without the participation signal, the measurements add nothing a linear model can use.
* **Among participants only** (participation fixed, 120 backtest rows), adding measurements changes AUC by +0.027 (+0.006 to +0.044) and Brier by -0.0108 (-0.0184 to -0.0030). This is the cleanest test of athletic information.
* The participation flag on its own ranks drafted vs undrafted QBs with AUC 0.863. An invitation is the NFL's own scouting verdict, published about two months before the draft.

So, in the full population, the "college + Combine" model mostly learns **what NFL scouts had already decided**, not how fast or big a QB is. Among QBs who were measured, the measurements do carry a small, real signal (the participant-only interval excludes zero), but too small to show up once the flag is in the model. Participation is still legitimate pre-draft information at its cutoff, but it should be presented as such: a post-invitation forecast, not evidence that athletic testing predicts the draft.

| participant, forty recorded | 164 | 0.579 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |
| participant, forty missing | 30 | 0.867 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 |

Measurements exist only for participants, so "missing forty" in the full population means "not invited". Among participants, skipping the forty is itself a signal (top prospects often skip drills), so even the participant-only model has informative missingness. Median imputation hides the flag from a linear model only partly.

## What changes in the system

* The forecast interface reports draft probability at **separate cutoffs**: `pre_combine` (college only, model A = `models/qb_draft/v1.0.0/prob_college_l2.joblib`) and `post_combine` (model C = `prob_college_combine_l2.joblib`). The post-Combine number is labelled as driven mainly by participation.
* A QB with no Combine record in a year whose Combine has not happened yet gets the pre-Combine number only. The interface never treats "no record yet" as "not invited".
* No model artifact was changed. The Phase-5 headline (AUC 0.907) stays correct as a post-Combine backtest number, but this audit changes what it means.
