# Calibration improvements (Phase 5.5)

Generated by `python -m src.modeling.phase5_5_report` from the nested backtests of `python -m src.modeling.career_production_v11` and `python -m src.modeling.round_audit`.

## Summary

* **Five-season intervals (direct models).** The Phase-5 method (`oof_abs`) covered on average 78% (pre-draft) and 73% (post-draft) at nominal 80%. The v1.1.0 intervals (one method per feature set, chosen inside development folds) cover 79% and 77%, with intervals 1.18x and 1.07x as wide.
* **Rushing** under-coverage is reduced but not removed (table below). v1.1.0 ships `oof_scaled` pre-draft and `oof_scaled` post-draft. Pre-draft is a reviewed override: the automated final choice was `quantile_reg`, which under-covered in every outer evaluation. The shipped method's measured 80% coverage is 79% pre-draft and 77% post-draft.
* **Structured simulation.** PIT recalibration moves 80% coverage from 88% to 87% (pre-draft) and 88% to 85% (post-draft). The simulation remains a secondary output.
* **Start probabilities** are now nested for every QB. The independent Phase-5 logits violated P(64+) <= P(32+) <= P(16+) for 16 of 107 test QBs pre-draft and 27 post-draft. The pool-adjacent-violators projection fixes that without changing Brier score or log loss in any material way.
* **No guarantee.** Conformal-style guarantees assume exchangeable data. Draft classes drift (rushing QBs, the 17-game season), so every coverage number here is an empirical measurement on later classes, not a guarantee.

* The proportional-odds model is competitive: post-draft it has the lowest Brier score at 32+ (0.108 vs 0.114) and 64+ (0.092 vs 0.100), but its calibration slopes are further from 1 at 16+ and 32+. It was not pre-specified as the default, the differences are within sampling noise for about 107 QBs, and the PAVA projection is the smaller change, so v1.1.0 keeps the projected logits. The ordinal model is the natural candidate once more classes are available.
* **64+ starts pre-draft is poorly calibrated** (slope 0.24; 15 events). Its predictions are too spread out: high predictions are too high. With so few events a recalibration fit would itself be unreliable, so none is applied. The interface reports it with a warning.
* Recalibration (Platt or isotonic) was not applied to any threshold. At 107 test QBs and 15-45 events per threshold, a fitted recalibration map would add more variance than it removes.
